隐私与数据

Women Wellness 默认将健康记录保存在设备端，不上传云端服务器。隐私政策请见你的公开隐私政策页面。

医疗边界

本 App 不提供医疗诊断，不替代医生、心理咨询师或其他专业人员。如果你出现严重异常出血、剧烈疼痛、胸痛、持续严重失眠、明显抑郁或自伤想法，请及时寻求专业帮助。

English

Women Wellness helps users record cycles, mood, sleep, and common symptoms, then generates gentle on-device trend feedback and suggestions. If you experience any issues while using the app, please contact us using the details below.

Common Support Topics

  • The app cannot open or crashes on launch
  • Face ID / Touch ID app lock issues
  • CSV or PDF export problems
  • Data deletion, privacy policy, or record explanations
  • Feature requests and product feedback

Contact Support

Support email: wasedchildren@gmail.com

Privacy and Data

Women Wellness stores health records locally on the device by default and does not upload them to cloud servers. Please refer to the public privacy policy page for details.

Medical Boundary

This app does not provide medical diagnosis and does not replace doctors, therapists, or other professionals. If you experience severe abnormal bleeding, intense pain, chest pain, prolonged severe insomnia, serious depressive symptoms, or self-harm thoughts, please seek professional support promptly.
