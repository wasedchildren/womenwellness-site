Women Wellness Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Updated: May 26, 2026 | 更新日期：2026 年 5 月 26 日

中文

Women Wellness 面向更年期前后女性，提供周期、情绪、睡眠和症状记录。我们优先保护敏感健康数据，默认仅在设备本地存储，不上传云端服务器。

1. 我们收集的数据

  • 周期记录：开始日期、结束日期、出血量、周期状态和备注。
  • 情绪记录：情绪标签、压力、焦虑、睡眠和精力评分。
  • 症状记录：潮热、夜间盗汗、睡眠问题、心悸、头痛等症状及严重程度。
  • 设置数据：应用锁、提醒偏好、绝经状态和本地 AI 次数。

2. 数据如何存储

  • 健康记录默认仅保存在当前设备。
  • 我们不会将周期、症状、情绪和备注上传到服务器。
  • 应用会尽量使用系统文件保护能力加强本地数据安全。

3. AI 建议说明

MVP 第一版的建议能力以本地规则和轻量模板为主，用于生成非诊断性的情绪调节建议、趋势提醒和就医提示。

  • 不提供医疗诊断。
  • 不替代医生、心理咨询师或其他专业人员。
  • 建议基于本机保存的数据生成。

4. 我们不会做的事情

  • 不出售用户健康数据。
  • 不用于广告画像或跨应用追踪。
  • 不在未经同意的情况下上传敏感健康记录。

5. 数据导出

如果用户主动导出 CSV 或 PDF 文件，导出后的文件由用户自行保存或分享。请在分享前确认其中是否包含敏感备注内容。

6. 安全提醒

如果记录中出现异常出血、剧烈疼痛、胸痛、持续严重失眠、明显抑郁或自伤相关表达，应用可能建议用户联系医生、心理咨询师或紧急支持资源。这些提醒不是诊断结果。

7. 联系方式

支持邮箱：wasedchildren@gmail.com

English

Women Wellness is designed for women in perimenopause and menopause to record cycles, mood, sleep, and symptoms. We prioritize sensitive health data protection. By default, data is stored only on the user’s device and is not uploaded to cloud servers.

1. Data We Collect

  • Cycle records: start date, end date, flow, cycle status, and notes.
  • Mood records: mood labels, stress, anxiety, sleep, and energy scores.
  • Symptom records: hot flashes, night sweats, sleep issues, palpitations, headaches, and other symptom severity details.
  • Settings data: app lock, reminder preferences, menopause stage, and local AI usage counts.

2. How Data Is Stored

  • Health records are stored locally on the current device by default.
  • We do not upload cycle, symptom, mood, or note data to our servers.
  • The app uses system-level file protection where possible to strengthen local data security.

3. AI Suggestion Notes

The first MVP version uses local rules and lightweight templates to provide non-diagnostic emotional regulation suggestions, trend reminders, and care prompts.

  • No medical diagnosis is provided.
  • The app does not replace doctors, therapists, or other professionals.
  • Suggestions are generated from data saved on the device.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell user health data.
  • We do not use data for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not upload sensitive health records without user consent.

5. Data Export

If users export CSV or PDF files, those files are stored or shared by the user. Please review whether exported notes contain sensitive information before sharing.

6. Safety Reminders

If records include abnormal bleeding, severe pain, chest pain, prolonged insomnia, major depressive symptoms, or self-harm related expressions, the app may suggest contacting a doctor, therapist, or emergency support. These reminders are not diagnostic results.
